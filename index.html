About

About me

I’m Bahodirjon — a graphic designer from Uzbekistan who turns ideas into clear, memorable brands. I believe every line in a logo should have a reason to be there.

My work starts with understanding the business: who it speaks to, what makes it different and where the brand will live. Then I sketch, test and refine until the idea is simple enough to remember.

The result is more than a logo — it is a complete visual language: colour, typography and rules that keep the brand consistent everywhere.

Services

What I do

  2. 02

  3. 03

  4. 04

Process

How I work

  1. 01

    Brief & research

    We talk about your business, audience and goals. I study the market and your competitors.

  2. 02

    Concepts

    I explore directions through sketches and present the strongest ideas with the reasoning behind them.

  3. 03

    Refinement

    We choose one direction and polish every detail: shape, colour and typography.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    You receive every file in the formats you need, plus simple guidelines for using them.
